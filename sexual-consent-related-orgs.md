### 🔬 NIH (National Institutes of Health)
- **PubMed / National Library of Medicine**: The primary database for peer-reviewed research on sexual consent. Key topics include alcohol's effect on consent perception, LGBTQ+ consent literature, and the RealConsent program.
    - [PubMed: Related Citations on Sexual Consent](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/m/pubmed.mobile/37606319/related/)

# Top Sources
## Tier 1: Highest Regard from Both Civilians and Authorities

### 🥇 National Coalition for Sexual Freedom (NCSF)

**Civilian regard:** NCSF has received multiple awards from leather and BDSM communities, including the Pantheon of Leather's Large Nonprofit Organization of the Year in 1999, 2002, 2005, and 2010. It operates as a coalition of over 140 partner groups, clubs, and businesses, making it the primary umbrella advocacy organization for BDSM, leather-fetish, swinging, and polyamory communities. Its Kink Aware Professionals (KAP) directory, with over 2,000 entries, is a trusted civilian resource for finding affirming therapists, medical providers, and legal professionals.

**Authoritative regard:** NCSF's authority recognition is the strongest in this space. It has filed amicus curiae briefs in courts including the U.S. Navy-Marine Corps Court of Criminal Appeals and the U.S. Court of Appeals. It educates law enforcement, victim advocacy agencies, state and local officials, and healthcare professionals. Its Consent Counts program has been working since 2007 to decriminalize consensual sexual conduct, and the American Law Institute has committed to publishing Explicit Prior Permission in 2025, enabling legislative efforts to decriminalize BDSM. NCSF also contributed to the DSM Revision Project that influenced the depathologization of certain practices in mental health diagnostics. Its Media Kit states that since its formation in 1997, NCSF and its programs have "significantly changed the way the media, legal and psychiatric professions view kink and nonmonogamy".

**Verdict:** NCSF is the single most highly regarded organization in this space by both civilians and authorities. It is the closest thing to an institutional voice for these communities in legal and policy contexts.

## Tier 2: Strong Authoritative Regard, Strong Civilian Presence

### 🥈 Center for Positive Sexuality

**Civilian regard:** Well-regarded in sex-positive and kink communities as a research and education nonprofit established in 2007. It publishes the *Journal of Positive Sexuality*, a peer-reviewed multidisciplinary journal whose articles were viewed or downloaded over 35,000 times worldwide in 2019.

**Authoritative regard:** This is where the Center stands out. It is an **AASECT-approved Continuing Education Provider**, listed among organizations such as the Ackerman Institute and Adler University. AASECT (American Association of Sexuality Educators, Counselors, and Therapists) is the primary professional credentialing body for sex therapists and educators in the U.S. The Center's Executive Director, Emily E. Prior, won the Vern Bullough Award for research, and its board includes academics from Idaho State University and other institutions. Its journal's editorial board includes faculty from the Mayo Clinic, University of North Carolina Charlotte, and University of Ottawa. The journal is explicitly "designed to be accessible and beneficial to a large and diverse readership, including academics, policymakers, clinicians, educators, and students".

**Verdict:** The Center for Positive Sexuality is arguably the most academically and professionally credentialed organization in this space. Its AASECT approval gives it a level of institutional legitimacy that few community organizations achieve.

### 🥉 The Eulenspiegel Society (TES) and Society of Janus

**Civilian regard:** TES is the oldest and largest BDSM education and support group in the United States, founded in 1971 in New York City. The Society of Janus is the second oldest BDSM organization in the U.S., founded in 1974 in San Francisco, and was inducted into the **Leather Hall of Fame in 2018**. Both are historically foundational and widely respected within the community.

**Authoritative regard:** Their longevity and historical significance give them a form of institutional recognition. The Leather Hall of Fame, which inducted the Society of Janus, was formed to "honor and preserve the legacy of the extraordinary men and women who have significantly impacted and shaped the history of the Leather/BDSM communities worldwide since 1950". TES and Janus are frequently cited in academic literature on BDSM history and community organization. However, neither has the same level of direct engagement with legal or governmental authorities as NCSF.

**Verdict:** Both are highly regarded as historical pillars of the community, but their authoritative recognition is primarily historical and cultural rather than legal or policy-oriented.

## Tier 3: Notable Regard, Primarily in Specific Domains

### Alternative Sexualities Health Research Alliance (TASHRA)

**Civilian regard:** Respected in kink communities for its focus on health research and its Kink and Flourishing Survey.

**Authoritative regard:** TASHRA is a registered 501(c)(3) research organization that has collaborated with **Gilead Sciences, Inc.** on a study of HIV Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP) uptake among kink-involved individuals. It is listed on **ClinicalTrials.gov**, the U.S. government registry of clinical trials. Its Research Director, Richard Sprott, Ph.D., is also on the editorial board of the *Journal of Positive Sexuality*. This gives TASHRA a degree of governmental and pharmaceutical-industry recognition that is rare in this space.

**Verdict:** TASHRA is the most authoritative organization specifically for kink health research, though its scope is narrower than NCSF or the Center for Positive Sexuality.

### Pan Eros Foundation / Consent Academy

**Civilian regard:** Pan Eros Foundation runs the Seattle Erotic Art Festival and the Consent Academy, which has trained thousands of people since 2016. It is a federally recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit registered with Washington's Secretary of State.

**Authoritative regard:** The 501(c)(3) status and state registration provide formal legal recognition. Consent Academy publishes *The Consent Primer*, a book authored by a team of educators and therapists. However, there is no evidence of direct engagement with courts, government agencies, or professional credentialing bodies comparable to NCSF or the Center for Positive Sexuality.

**Verdict:** A well-regarded educational organization with formal nonprofit recognition, but its authoritative reach is primarily local and educational rather than legal or policy-oriented.

### Sex Positive World

**Civilian regard:** An umbrella organization with nearly 7,000 members across 5 countries and 10 chapters in the U.S. and Europe. It is entirely volunteer-run and has a strong community presence.

**Authoritative regard:** It is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit and has presented at **AASECT 2025**, indicating some professional recognition. Its guiding principles are explicitly inspired by the **World Health Organization's** definition of sexuality, sexual health, and sexual rights.

**Verdict:** Strong civilian community organization with emerging professional recognition, but less authoritative engagement than Tier 1 or 2 organizations.
