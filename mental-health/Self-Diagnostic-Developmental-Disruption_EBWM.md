# Symptoms of Trauma by Developmental Age Range (Chakra Model)

Based on Anodea Judith's *Eastern Body, Western Mind*, each chakra corresponds to a developmental stage, and trauma during that stage produces characteristic symptoms. Below is a synthesis organized by age range.

## Chakra 1 — Womb to ~12 Months (Infancy)
**Core issue:** Right to be here / survival, grounding, trust

**Symptoms of trauma at this stage:**
- Disconnection from the body; numbness; dissociation
- Hypervigilance, restlessness, anxiety, inability to relax or settle
- Chronic fear; feeling unsafe in the world; mistrust
- Poor focus and discipline; chronic disorganization
- Financial difficulty; problems with health, money, housing, jobs
- Poor boundaries (or overly rigid boundaries)
- Underweight or, in excess, obesity/overeating, hoarding, materialism
- Frequent illness; eating disorders; disorders of bones, teeth, bowel, legs, feet
- Difficulty with containment, grounding, and feeling "real"
- Upper-chakra dominance: living in the head, neglect of basic needs (eating, resting)
- In severe cases: schizoid withdrawal, feeling unwanted, questioning one's right to exist

---

## Chakra 2 — ~6 Months to 2 Years
**Core issue:** Right to feel and have pleasure; movement, emotion, sexuality

**Symptoms of trauma at this stage:**
- Emotional numbness; deadened senses; loss of appetite for food, sex, life
- Rigidity in body and attitudes; fear of change; lack of desire/passion/excitement
- Fear of sex; frigidity; shame around pleasure
- Poor social skills; excessive boundaries; isolation
- OR excess: sexual acting out, pleasure addiction, emotional volatility, hysteria, bipolar mood swings, crisis junkiness, poor boundaries, invasion of others, seductive manipulation, emotional dependency, obsessive attachment
- Guilt (the "demon" of this chakra) dominating experience
- Difficulty knowing what one wants; disconnection between feeling self and external image
- Low back pain, knee trouble, lack of flexibility; menstrual/sexual dysfunction
- In severe abuse: dissociation, repressed/intrusive memories, confusion between pleasure and pain, difficulty with intimacy and trust, celibacy or promiscuity

---

## Chakra 3 — ~18 Months to 4 Years
**Core issue:** Right to act; autonomy, will, self-esteem

**Symptoms of trauma at this stage:**
- **Deficiency:** low energy, weak will, poor self-discipline, low self-esteem, coldness (emotional/physical), poor digestion, collapsed middle, victim mentality, blaming others, passivity, unreliability
- **Excess:** overly aggressive, dominating, controlling, need to be right/have the last word, manipulative, power-hungry, deceitful, temper tantrums, violent outbursts, stubbornness, type-A drive, competitiveness, arrogance, hyperactivity
- Shame (the "demon" of this chakra): collapsed third chakra, collapsed posture, restricted breathing, feeling "fatally flawed"
- Eating disorders, ulcers, hypoglycemia, diabetes, chronic fatigue, hypertension, stomach/pancreas/gallbladder/liver disorders
- Passive-aggressive behavior; feeling stuck; sabotaging oneself
- Difficulty with impulse control; either submission or rebellion to authority
- Parentified children: chronic sense of inadequacy from premature responsibility

---

## Chakra 4 — ~4 to 7 Years
**Core issue:** Right to love and be loved; social identity, relationships

**Symptoms of trauma at this stage:**
- **Deficiency:** antisocial, withdrawn, cold, critical/judgmental, loneliness, isolation, depression, fear of intimacy/relationships, lack of empathy, narcissism
- **Excess:** codependency, poor boundaries, demanding, clinging, jealousy, overly sacrificing
- Heart/lung/thymus/breast/arm disorders; shortness of breath; sunken chest; circulation problems; asthma; immune deficiency; tension between shoulder blades; chest pain
- Twisted concepts of love: equating love with pain/abuse; the "fantasy bond"; accepting mistreatment
- Rejection sensitivity; identification with the rejector; self-rejection
- Rigid/Achiever pattern: disconnected from feelings, driven achievement, fear of surrender, never feeling "enough"
- Hysteric pattern: uneven, sporadic emotional expression; demanding/possessive; longing for authenticity through strong emotion

---

## Chakra 5 — ~7 to 12 Years
**Core issue:** Right to speak and hear truth; creativity, self-expression

**Symptoms of trauma at this stage:**
- **Deficiency:** fear of speaking, small/weak voice, difficulty putting feelings into words, introversion, shyness, tone deafness, poor rhythm
- **Excess:** too much talking (as defense), inability to listen, poor auditory comprehension, gossiping, dominating voice, interrupting
- Throat/ear/voice/neck disorders; tightness of the jaw; toxicity
- Secrets and lies: guarded expression, difficulty with intimacy, internalized silence
- Internalized harsh inner critic regulating speech
- Difficulty listening to others or entertaining new ideas (from yelling/authoritarian environments)
- Challenger-Defender pattern: charismatic but poorly grounded, rage and distrust in relationships, hiding vulnerability behind a powerful persona

---

## Chakra 6 — Adolescence
**Core issue:** Right to see; identity, intuition, pattern recognition

**Symptoms of trauma at this stage:**
- **Deficiency:** insensitivity, poor vision, poor memory, difficulty seeing the future, lack of imagination, difficulty visualizing, denial, monopolarized thinking ("one true right way")
- **Excess:** hallucinations, delusions, obsessions, difficulty concentrating, nightmares
- Headaches; vision problems
- Repressed memories affecting psychic life; dissociation from painful material
- Invalidation of intuition and psychic experience
- Inability to separate love from abuse (when what is seen contradicts what is told)
- Shame-based aversion to being seen; inability to meet another's gaze ("psychological blindness")
- Identity vs. role confusion; cluttered psyche from unacknowledged patterns

---

## Chakra 7 — Early Adulthood and Beyond
**Core issue:** Right to know; belief systems, spirituality, universal identity

**Symptoms of trauma at this stage:**
- **Deficiency:** spiritual cynicism, learning difficulties, rigid belief systems, apathy, materialism/greed/domination (excess in lower chakras)
- **Excess:** overintellectualization, spiritual addiction, confusion, dissociation from body
- Coma, migraines, brain tumors, amnesia, cognitive delusions
- Cognitive closure: refusal to accept new knowledge; need to be right
- Self-fulfilling beliefs about limitations ("I can't," "It's impossible")
- Effects of withheld information, forced religiosity, invalidation of beliefs, blind obedience, misinformation, spiritual abuse
- Difficulty trusting a higher power; inability to surrender

---

## Cross-Cutting Notes from the Text

- **Trauma forces energy upward** — cutting off bodily sensation and producing upper-chakra dominance regardless of the age of the trauma.
- **Symptoms can be excess or deficiency** in any chakra; the same trauma may produce either, depending on coping strategy.
- **Later chakras are affected by earlier damage** — e.g., first-chakra damage weakens power (3rd), relationships (4th), and communication (5th).
- **Inherited trauma** (parents' survival fears, unresolved grief, untreated abuse) can contaminate a chakra even without direct abuse.
- **Character structures** (Schizoid, Oral, Endurer, Rigid, Challenger-Defender) represent chronic, body-level trauma adaptations tied to specific developmental windows.
